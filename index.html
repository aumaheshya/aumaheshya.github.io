        Academic Profile

        I believe intelligent systems should think with us, not for us. My ambition is simple: that the support technology offers today can become a capability we carry tomorrow.

        At the intersection of humans, computers and their interactions, I explore Cognitive, Affective and Agentic Incorporation as an XR Design Space, designing around how we perceive and remember, how we feel, and what we choose and control. Blending the real and the virtual, XR can engage many routes into memory at once: what we imagine, see, hear and touch, how we move, what we feel, and where we are. So if one route weakens, through ageing, a neurocognitive condition, stress or distraction, another can still lead the way back.

        Combining XR with AI, biosensing, psychology and memory research, I build adaptive systems for skill learning, attention and neurocognitive support, guided by one question: how can adaptive XR strengthen human capabilities rather than replace them?
