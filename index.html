      Academic Profile

      I work at the meeting point of people, computers, and the interactions between them. My research explores Cognitive (mental processing, attention, and memory) and Affective (emotions, moods, and social presence) Incorporation as an XR Design Space: designing technology that helps people by adapting to them. I build and study extended reality (AR/VR/MR) systems that use AI and biosensing to understand the person using them and adapt to their needs, with applications in skill learning, attention and distraction, and neurocognitive impairments.
